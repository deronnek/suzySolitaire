# Suze Solitaire Privacy Policy

Suze Solitaire doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

- The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- The only time it goes online is to talk to Apple's App Store about the optional Unlimited Undo purchase: to show its price, to buy or restore it, and to check whether you own it. Apple handles the payment; the app never sees your payment details or Apple Account.
- Your game in progress, score, wins, losses and settings are saved only on your device.
- If you choose a photo for the card back, you pick it with the system photo picker, which gives the app only that one photo. A copy is kept on your device for the card back and is never sent anywhere. Choosing a different back or deleting the app removes it.

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/deronnek/suzySolitaire/issues.
