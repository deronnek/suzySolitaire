# Suze Solitaire Privacy Policy

This policy covers Suze Solitaire on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Suze Solitaire doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

- The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- The only time it goes online is to talk to your app store about the optional Unlimited Undo purchase. That's Apple's App Store on iPhone and iPad, and Google Play on Android. The app uses it to show the price, to buy the purchase or bring it back, and to check whether you own it. Apple or Google handles the payment, and the app never sees your payment details or your Apple or Google account.
- Your game in progress, score, wins, losses and settings are saved only on your device. If you've turned on your device's own backup (iCloud Backup, or Google's backup on Android), they're included in that backup like other apps' data. The app itself never sends them anywhere.
- If you choose a photo for the card back, you pick it with the system photo picker, which gives the app only that one photo. A copy is kept on your device for the card back, and like your game it can be part of your device's own backup. The app never sends it anywhere. Choosing a different back or deleting the app removes it.

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/deronnek/suzySolitaire/issues.
