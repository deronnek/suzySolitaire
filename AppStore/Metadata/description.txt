Suze Solitaire is a classic Canfield-style solitaire, built for quick games on iPhone and iPad.

Thirteen cards start face up in the blind beside the deck. One card is dealt to the upper stacks, and its rank starts all four of them. Build the upper stacks up by suit, wrapping from King to Ace, and build the four lower stacks down in alternating colors. Deal from the deck three cards at a time, as often as you like. Each card you put up earns $5, and a win earns $250.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Drag cards and runs, or double-tap a card to send it to an upper stack
• Tap anywhere on the table to deal
• Undo your last move, or undo New Game if you tapped it by mistake
• Unlimited Undo, an optional in-app purchase, takes back as many moves as you like, even auto moves
• Auto Moves puts cards up for you as soon as they can go
• Ask for a Hint when you're stuck

OPTIONAL HELP, OFF UNTIL YOU WANT IT
• Highlight Drop Spot outlines where a card will land as you drag it
• Show When Stuck tells you when only the deck has something to play, or when the game can't be won

MADE FOR YOUR DEVICE
• Portrait and landscape on iPhone and iPad, including Split View and Slide Over
• Dark mode with a dimmer table
• Choose your card artwork, and pick any photo as your card back
• Gentle haptics as you pick up and drag cards
• Full VoiceOver support, with actions to select and move cards
• Your game, score, wins and losses are saved automatically

No ads, no accounts, and no data collected. Your game never leaves your device.
