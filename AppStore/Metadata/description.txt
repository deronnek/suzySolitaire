Suze Solitaire is Canfield-style patience with a money score that carries over from game to game. Every new game costs $45, every card you put up earns $5, and a win pays $250. Can you stay out of the red?

It's named for Aunt Suze, who learned this game as a child.

HOW IT PLAYS
Thirteen cards start face up in the blind beside the deck. One card is dealt to the upper stacks, and its rank starts all four of them, so every game builds from a different card. Build the upper stacks up by suit, wrapping from King to Ace, and the four lower stacks down in alternating colors. Empty lower stacks fill from the blind while it lasts. Deal from the deck three cards at a time, as often as you like.

DEALS YOU CAN WIN
Turn on Winnable Deals Only and every new game is one you can win. A computer player tried thousands of deals ahead of time, seeing only the cards you can see and never taking a move back, and we kept only the ones it won. If you get stuck, it's the play and not the cards.

ORIGINAL CARD ARTWORK
Two decks designed for Suze Solitaire, each with its own card back:
• Watercolor: Art Nouveau cards in soft, layered washes with gold fans in the corners, and a Matterhorn card back painted from real Swiss terrain data
• Café Racer: bold Bauhaus-style cards in flat color, with a rider on a café racer motorcycle on the back
Or play with Classic or Classic Large, whose big corner ranks are easy to read on a phone.

TRACK HOW YOU'RE DOING
Tap the score to open Stats:
• Wins, losses, win rate, and your current and best streaks
• Total money, your best and worst games, and cards put up per game
• Average moves per game and per win, and your fewest moves in a win
• Average time per game and your fastest win, counting only time spent playing

UNDO, YOUR WAY
• Undo your last move for free, or undo New Game if you tapped it by mistake
• Unlimited Undo, an optional one-time purchase, steps back through every move to the start of the game, even auto moves
• Swipe in from the left edge to undo, or from the right edge for a hint

HELP WHEN YOU WANT IT
• Hint points out a move, or tells you to deal
• Auto Moves puts cards up for you as soon as they can go
• Show When Stuck lets you know when only the deck has a play, or when the game can't be won, once you've stopped to look
• Highlight Drop Spot outlines where a card will land as you drag it
All of these, and Winnable Deals Only, are off until you turn them on.
