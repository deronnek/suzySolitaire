2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Suze Solitaire is a single-player Canfield-style solitaire for anyone who enjoys a quick game of patience: offline, with no ads, accounts or data collection, full VoiceOver support, larger text and dark mode, on every iPhone and iPad size.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
This is not a template or a reskin, and no other solitaire games come from this account. It's named for our Aunt Suze, who learned this game as a child, and was written for this one game:
- Dealing, with three choices: Totally Random; Winnable in Theory, the 2,952 of 3,400 deals our solver for these rules won knowing every card; and Winnable with Cunning, the 1,514 of 5,400 won by our computer player that sees only what a person sees and never undoes a move. Our tests replay the winning moves under the app's own rules.
- Original artwork: two decks drawn for this app, each with its own back. Watercolor has Art Nouveau cards in layered washes and a Matterhorn back rendered from real Swiss terrain data; Café Racer has Bauhaus-style cards and a motorcycle rider on the back.
- The rules are a specific variant: a 13-card blind, upper stacks that start from the rank dealt and wrap from King to Ace, and empty lower stacks only the blind can fill until it runs out.
- A running money score carries over between games ($45 per game, $5 per card up, $250 per win). No real money, wagering or prizes.
- Stats (tap the score) tracks wins, losses, streaks, best and worst games, and moves and time per game.
- Undo can take back New Game; Unlimited Undo steps back through every move, even automatic ones.
- Show When Stuck works out whether any move is left, even deep in the deck.

3. HOW TO USE IT
No login, setup or sample files are needed; a game is dealt at launch.
- Tap the deck (top left) or the table to deal three cards. Drag cards and runs, or double-tap a card to send it up. Rules: ••• menu > How to Play.
- Top corners: New Game (left) and Undo (right, free for the last move).
- ••• menu (bottom right): Hint, How to Play, Stats, Auto Moves, Show When Stuck, Highlight Drop Spot, Dealing (applies from the next New Game), Cards (Artwork: Classic, Classic Large, Watercolor, Café Racer; Back: a photo from the library), Unlimited Undo, Restore Purchase, About.
- Swipe in from the left edge to undo, or the right edge for a hint.
- Paid content: the one In-App Purchase, "Unlimited Undo" (non-consumable, product unlimited_undo), in the ••• menu, with "Restore Purchase" below it.
- A long press on the score shows a code the developer uses to turn on a test menu on our own devices. It needs a key only the developer has; there is no hidden content for users.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None besides Apple's frameworks; the app makes no network requests of its own. StoreKit handles the purchase and the system photo picker (PHPicker) an optional card-back photo, which stays on the device. No analytics, ads, accounts, AI or third-party SDKs.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None; only the purchase's local price differs. English only.
